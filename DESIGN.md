# Yubie digital design system

## Brand expression

Yubie balances premium editorial restraint with the energy of Indonesian produce. The experience should feel tactile, warm, modern, and credible—not clinical and not generic marketplace UI.

| Token | Value | Primary role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Yubie Purple | `#6417A8` | Signature fields, calls to action, energetic accents |
| Root Green | `#197A32` | Ingredient provenance and natural cues |
| Warm Gold | `#D9A72E` | Premium detail, focus states, highlights |
| Nourish White | `#FFFDF7` | Warm editorial canvas |
| Root Ink | `#24152E` | Typography and deep surfaces |

Display typography uses Cormorant Garamond for expressive phrases and Manrope for clear interface communication. Large headings may use tight tracking and compact leading; body copy must remain readable and conversational.

## Experience principles

1. **Product first.** Photography and product truth lead every conversion path.
2. **Editorial rhythm.** Alternate immersive imagery, strong typography, whitespace, and concise explanations.
3. **Honest commerce.** Coming-soon products never expose an active purchase action.
4. **Local, not nostalgic.** Celebrate Indonesian roots through contemporary composition rather than decorative stereotypes.
5. **Accessible by default.** Keyboard-visible focus, meaningful image alternatives, semantic controls, reduced layout shift, and responsive touch targets are non-negotiable.

## Photography

Use real supplied product and lifestyle assets. Prefer natural light, cream stone, deep purple textile, green leaves, and warm gold details. Avoid fabricated packaging labels, false certification badges, or image text that conflicts with verified catalog data.
