0, cs, admin, manusia, customer service, operator, bicara dengan admin, hubungi tim,
hubungi cs → HUMAN_HANDOFF. 9, kembali, back → current parent. Aliases also accept
case/spacing/punctuation normalization from normalizer.ts. Bounded product grammar:
(beli|harga|info|mau|produk) [yubie] (tepung|flour|shake|ppang|mie); tepung → flour.
Only numeric 1–4 in PRODUCT_MENU assigns by position. No customer-derived URL.

Product selection requires published available catalog variants/prices and trusted
PDP. Final approved photo → SEND_PRODUCT_MEDIA, otherwise honest text fallback.
PRODUCT_DETAIL choice 1 only describes purchase when checkout_available; otherwise
catalog guidance. Missing catalog capability suppresses website links. ORDER_PORTAL
requires account_available AND order_portal_available. Payment guidance respects
checkout_available. Promo requires effective approved code/percentage/eligibility
matching the existing domain quote; absent/expired/mismatched evidence is neutral.

B2B_MENU 1–4 selects bulk/reseller/sample-development/partnership. Consent 1 starts
company → need → product → city → volume; each answer ≤256 characters. Volume may
be lewati. Consent 2 routes Sales with no collected summary. No promises of MOQ or
commercial approval. Global navigation remains available during collection.

Routing order: ownership/emergency policy; silent safety exception; explicit human
or health/order/refund escalation; global commands; state choices; bounded product aliases;
fallback. Safety patterns are defined in aliases.ts (adverse reaction, reaksi buruk/
alergi, contamination, poison, spoilage, mold, unusual smell, foreign object, damaged
packaging, vomiting, abdominal pain). No medical answer is generated.

Ownership is separate from navigation: BOT_ELIGIBLE → BOT_ACTIVE; handoff commits
HANDOFF_REQUESTED and ordered effects; confirmed routing → QUEUED; provider owner
→ HUMAN_ACTIVE. HUMAN_ACTIVE/HANDOFF_REQUESTED/QUEUED/RESOLVED suppress customer
replies. Food safety still creates internal FOOD_SAFETY_HANDOFF without a reply.
Closure → sticky RESOLVED, including signed article-less state events. Reopening
does not release it. Operator release after ≥30 minutes, provider owner cleared,
ticket AUTO, then customer menu/start are all required for BOT_ELIGIBLE.

Unknown input preserves node, increments durable fallbackCount: first offers valid
choices; second offers menu/CS; third requests support. Empty unsupported media
uses the unsupported copy with the same bound. FALLBACK is a counter, not a saved
node. BOT_ELIGIBLE/HUMAN_ACTIVE are ownership states, not navigation nodes.

V1→V2 or unknown node: reset context/count to HOME and show migration notice;
do not consume the old numeric choice. Safety/human requests retain precedence.
Failed earlier inbox rows block newer navigation until retry; after five attempts
an idempotent internal recovery handoff suppresses navigation, retaining the failed
row for operator recovery. Late old navigation resets to HOME without interpreting
its number against a new menu; late human/health/safety requests still escalate.

Typed effects: REPLY_TEXT, SHOW_MENU, SHOW_APPROVED_FACT, SEND_WEBSITE_LINK,
SEND_PRODUCT_MEDIA, CREATE_HANDOFF, SUPPRESS_REPLY, RESET_NAVIGATION. They carry
validated payloads. The worker commits state/audit/outbox/inbox completion together;
provider effects occur after commit. There is no commerce mutation action.
