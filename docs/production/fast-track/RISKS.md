# Material release risks

The production preview remains different from this candidate. Staging Access prevents deployed SHA/digest and fresh Google verification. Source-level and local browser passes cannot close that gap.

Product label/specification approval, email delivery, official marketplace identities and legal approval are owner facts. The public artifact omits unapproved specifications and direct payment flows; this reduces launch scope without inventing approvals.

The local static server interprets emitted Vercel routes for acceptance. Real Vercel platform behavior and actual resource/header delivery still require authorized staging deployment and tests. Artifact sourceDirty must be false for remote acceptance.

ERPNext installation is only a prerequisite. Synthetic local accounts and item mappings do not approve production RBAC, UOMs, BOMs, shelf life, finance or stock authority. Quarantine requires enforced native permissions/workflow and operator UAT; names on warehouses alone cannot ensure safety. Commerce ERP stock writes remain refused until atomic authority, reconciliation and acceptance gates pass.

Rollback must not restore the observed preview checkout to a catalog launch. No financial or shared infrastructure change is authorized by a green catalog test.
