# 24 — Marketplace/WhatsApp Phase Plan

## M0 — Correct the product

ADR, channel IA, hide/de-emphasize cart/checkout, marketplace listing inventory, WhatsApp Business ownership.

## M1 — Purchase routing

Listing registry, purchase options, tracked redirect, WhatsApp CTA, listing health and channel analytics.

## M2 — Conversation operations

Chatwoot, WhatsApp Cloud API, routing/business hours, signed webhook inbox and human handoff. No LLM auto-reply before this works.

## M3 — Safe chatbot

Assistant package, intent taxonomy, approved knowledge, tools, policy, evaluation and bot/human state machine. Roll out shadow -> suggested reply -> low-risk auto-reply.

## M4 — B2B CRM

Qualification, durable lead signal, owner/SLA, replaceable CRM adapter and follow-up measurement.

## M5 — Marketplace intelligence

Official seller exports first: sales, SKU mapping, cancellation/return and dashboards.

## M6 — Marketplace APIs

Only after approved access and measured manual-work reduction. Read before write.

## M7 — ERPNext

Only when physical operations justify it.

## M8 — Direct checkout

Only after a new ADR.

Every phase closes with problem, outcome, canonical owner, failure modes, security/privacy, tests, operator workflow, rollback and GO/NO-GO.
